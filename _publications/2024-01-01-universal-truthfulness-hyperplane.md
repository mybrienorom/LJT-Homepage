This work investigates the universal truthfulness hyperplane inside large language models. I am the first author, and it was published at EMNLP 2024. Co-authors are Shiqi Chen, Yu Cheng, and Junxian He. Code is available on GitHub (Universal_Truthfulness_Hyperplane).
