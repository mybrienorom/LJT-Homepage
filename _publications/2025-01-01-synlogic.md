SynLogic synthesizes verifiable reasoning data at scale for learning logical reasoning and beyond. I am the first author of this work, which was published on Arxiv in 2025. Co-authors are Yuanxiang Fan, Zhuo Jiang, Han Ding, Yongyi Hu, Chi Zhang, Yiqi Shi, Shitong Weng, Aili Chen, Shiqi Chen, Yunan Huang, Mozhi Zhang, Pengyu Zhao, Junjie Yan, and Junxian He. Code is available on GitHub.
