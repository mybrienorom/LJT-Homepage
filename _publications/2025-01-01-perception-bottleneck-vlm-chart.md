This work studies the perception bottleneck of Vision-Language Models (VLMs) for chart understanding. I am the first author, and it was published on Arxiv in 2025. Co-authors are Weihao Zeng, Xiwen Zhang, Yijun Wang, Zifei Shan, and Junxian He. Code is available on GitHub (Vision4Chart).
